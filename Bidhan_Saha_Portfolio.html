About me

I am a Computer Science and Engineering undergraduate at AIUB and an AI/ML researcher with hands-on experience in deep learning, computer vision and medical imaging. I develop and optimize YOLO frameworks, ResNet architectures and Vision Transformers (ViT) for real-time object detection and classification, including medical diagnostic models for pneumonia and lumbar spine level detection.

I collaborate with senior AIUB faculty members on research across deep learning, human-computer interaction (HCI) and the Internet of Things (IoT). My work covers end-to-end dataset pipelines, mathematical modeling and model evaluation, and I write research papers for peer-reviewed international journals and IEEE/Springer conferences.

My goal is to build scalable, intelligent and human-centered AI systems for real-world applications.
